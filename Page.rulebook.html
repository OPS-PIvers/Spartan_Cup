Everything you need to know about The Spartan Cup competition

photo_camera Photo Submission

Photo Requirements:

  • Photos must be taken during the game (not before or after)
  • Must include your face
  • If dressing for a theme, show your outfit

How to Submit:

  • Use the link in the @ohs_spartancup Instagram
  • Indicate which game you are attending
  • List who you are attending with
  • If necessary, explain how your outfit fits the theme (skip if obvious, e.g., wearing black for "blackout")

warning Misconduct & Cheating

❌ Actions Considered Cheating:

  • Reusing old photos for new games/events
  • Pretending to be at a game/event that you are not

⚠️ Actions Considered Misconduct:

  • Being thrown out of a game for unsportsmanlike behavior
  • Being banned/suspended from events and/or school

block Consequences of Cheating or Misconduct:

Anyone found to be guilty of cheating or misconduct will be DISQUALIFIED from the competition.
