Photo Submission

expand_more

All event participation must be verified with a photo. Photos must be clear, well-lit, and include you at the event location. Submissions must be made within 48 hours of the event's conclusion.

Photo Requirements:

  • Photos must be taken during the game (not before or after)
  • Must include your face
  • If dressing for a theme, show your outfit

How to Submit:

  • Use the link in the @ohs_spartancup Instagram
  • Indicate which game you are attending
  • List who you are attending with
  • If necessary, explain how your outfit fits the theme

Points System

expand_more

Earn points by attending events and collecting badges. Your total points determine your rank in The Spartan Cup.

Base Points: Each game/event starts with a base value of 10 points. Additional points can be earned through themed attendance, spotlight games, and badge collection.

Badge Bonuses: Collect badges to earn bonus points and multipliers. Each badge category (Participation, Variety, Loyalty, etc.) has its own point values and tier-based multipliers.

Misconduct & Cheating

expand_more

Any form of cheating, including submitting false photos or misrepresenting attendance, will result in immediate disqualification from The Spartan Cup and potential disciplinary action from the school.

❌ Actions Considered Cheating:

  • Reusing old photos for new games/events
  • Pretending to be at a game/event that you are not
  • Submitting photos taken by others

⚠️ Actions Considered Misconduct:

  • Being thrown out of a game for unsportsmanlike behavior
  • Being banned/suspended from events and/or school
  • Inappropriate behavior at school events
